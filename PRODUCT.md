# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Families and individuals in the city looking for a GP practice they can trust. Typically a parent booking for a child, an adult arranging their own check-up, or someone booking for an older relative. They often arrive on a phone, between other tasks, wanting to see a real open time and book it in under a minute.

## Product Purpose
HowBody is a family and general practice clinic. The site does two jobs:
1. It makes a first impression of a calm, competent, human practice through real people, real services and clear doctors.
2. It lets visitors book a real appointment slot on the spot, then see and cancel their bookings.

Success means a visitor books without calling, and trusts the clinic enough to come in.

## Brand Personality
Warm, unhurried and competent. The voice is plain and kind, like a good GP explaining something: short sentences, no jargon, no hype. The emotions to evoke are reassurance and ease. It should feel friendly without being childish, and medical without feeling clinical.

## Anti-references
- Sterile hospital blue-and-white stock sites with gloved hands and syringes up front.
- SaaS-style health-tech landing pages: purple gradients, floating app mockups, metric dashboards.
- Cream-and-brass "wellness spa" aesthetics.
- Busy portals with dense menus and badges.

## Design Principles
1. **Real people first.** Show clinicians and patients, not equipment or abstractions.
2. **Booking is never more than one tap away.** Live slots live on the home page, and every CTA leads there.
3. **Say less, kindly.** Short, plain copy. No invented statistics or marketing filler.
4. **Calm confidence.** One committed brand color, generous space, soft shapes and no visual noise.
5. **Trust through honesty.** Error, empty and loading states explain what happened and what to do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA:
- Body text contrast of 4.5:1 or better on every surface, including over the teal and dark sections.
- Visible focus, full keyboard support for the booking dialog, the doctor accordion and the testimonial controls.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: no entrance animation, instant state changes.
- Users span all ages, including older patients, so interactive text is at least 16px with generous tap targets.
